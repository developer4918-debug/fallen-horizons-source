05 / GOOD QUESTIONS

BEFORE
YOU BUILD.

Who can participate?+

Fallen Horizons is for young creators under 18. Participation is solo: one developer per project.

Does it cost anything?+

No. Registration is free and the hackathon is completely online.

Can I participate with a team?+

This edition is solo only. You'll create and submit your own project.

When is it, and how do I submit?+

The final schedule, rules, and submission instructions will be shared soon. Join the WhatsApp community to receive updates.

What can I build?+

Choose Web Development for a website or web app, or Game Development for a playable game. Detailed track rules will be announced with the submission guidelines.
